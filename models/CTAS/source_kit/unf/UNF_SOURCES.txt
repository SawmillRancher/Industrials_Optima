## Section-by-section citations
1. **UNF annual figures (FY2021–FY2025)**
   - **Income statement and cash flow:** the Consolidated Statements of Income and Cash Flows in the most recent 10-K that shows each year.
     - FY23–FY25: UNF_10K_FY25, pp.32 and 36.
     - FY22: UNF_10K_FY24.
     - FY21: UNF_10K_FY23.
   - **Cash and short-term investments:** the balance sheet in the 10-K for that fiscal year-end.
   - **Debt:** zero in every year. This comes from MD&A Liquidity in each 10-K ("no outstanding borrowings").
   - **Segments, new 3-segment structure (FY23–FY25):** UNF_10K_FY25, Note 15.
   - **Segments, old Core Laundry / Specialty Garments / First Aid structure:**
     - FY21–FY23: UNF_10K_FY23, Note 15.
     - FY24: UNF_8K_Q4FY24, Adjusted EBITDA table.
   - **Adjusted EBITDA:**
     - FY24–FY25: UNF_8K_Q4FY25 reconciliation.
     - FY23: UNF_8K_Q4FY24 reconciliation.
     - FY22: only unadjusted EBITDA (240.251) was reported, in UNF_8K_Q4FY23.
     - FY21: no EBITDA was reported. This is stated in the JSON, not estimated.
2. **FY2026 quarters:** the Q1, Q2 and Q3 earnings releases (income statement, segment table and GAAP-to-non-GAAP reconciliation). Prior-year quarters come from the comparative columns.
   - **Merger costs:**
     - Q2: $1.984m added back for proxy and merger costs (UNF_8K_Q2FY26).
     - Q3: $20.7m of Cintas transaction-related costs in SG&A (UNF_8K_Q3FY26 and UNF_10Q_Q3FY26).
   - **Q4 FY2026 / FY2026 10-K:** not filed on EDGAR as of 2026-10-06.
3. **Shares**
   - **Cover page:** UNF_10Q_Q3FY26 (14,532,908 Common and 3,551,265 Class B at 2026-07-01).
   - **Equity awards:** Merger Agreement §3.2 (Annex A of CTAS_424B3) and the S-4 Ex.107 fee table.
   - **CTAS shares to be issued:**
     - 14,261,683 is the registered number, based on share-equivalents as of 2026-04-16.
     - 14,071k is the pro forma figure at 2026-02-28.
   - **Cash consideration:** $2,825.669m (pro forma Note 3).
4. **Pro forma:** CTAS_424B3, pp.107–125 (Notes 1–6). The S-4 shows the same figures. No S-4/A was filed.
   - **No step-up on inventories or rental garments.** Only buildings and land were revalued (+$263.05m).
   - **Intangibles:**
     - Customer relationships: $1,241m, 15-year life.
     - Trade names: $50m, 3-year life.
   - **Tax rate:** 25%.
   - **Financing assumption:** $2.8bn at 5%.
5. **Financing**
   - **Bridge:** $2.85bn 364-day facility in CTAS_8K_DEAL Item 8.01. After the revolver replaced one tranche, $1.6bn remains (CTAS_424B3).
   - **Revolver:** $2.0bn, SOFR+70–114bp, matures 2031-03-27, with $1.25bn certain-funds (CTAS_8K_RCF and CTAS_10K_FY26 Note 6).
   - **Not on EDGAR through 2026-10-06:** no notes offering, term loan or bridge draw.
6. **CTAS FY26 10-K**
   - **Transaction costs:** $16.1m in MD&A and the segment table.
   - **Bridge fees:** $5.2m prepaid (Note 6).
   - **Termination fee:** $350m reverse fee in Risk Factors.
   - **Debt:** Note 6 (notes outstanding, maturities, no CP).
   - **Later quarter:** Q1 FY27 transaction costs of $14.4m are in CTAS_8K_Q1FY27.
